Privacy Policy

Top Qualifiers uploader app · last updated 3 October 2026

What this app is

The Top Qualifiers uploader is a private tool run by the owner of the Top Qualifiers YouTube channel on their own computer. It uploads the channel's own videos, sets their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and playlists, and reads the channel's own statistics. It is not offered to the public, and no other person signs in to it.

Google data it uses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app is allowed to:

  • upload and manage videos, playlists, channel details and comments on the owner's channel (YouTube Data API scopes youtube.upload and youtube.force-ssl);
  • read the channel's own analytics, such as views and watch time (yt-analytics.readonly).

It uses this access only to run the owner's channel. It does not access any other channel or any other user's data.

Where data is kept

The sign-in token and the channel statistics the app reads are stored only on the channel owner's own computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's own YouTube and YouTube Analytics services.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer any Google user data to anyone, and does not use it for advertising. Its use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; the stored token can then be deleted from the owner's computer.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the Top Qualifiers YouTube channel.
